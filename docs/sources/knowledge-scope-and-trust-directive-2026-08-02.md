# Confirmed Direction

- Verification Knowledge applicability scope and its trust or verification state are independent dimensions.
- User, Team, Project, Main/Deri, Customer, Product, and Global contexts do not form a quality ranking.
- Knowledge may apply to one or several contexts and retain its original owner and Source provenance.
- Draft, Candidate, Validated, Verified, and Deprecated are candidate operational Trust States; their exact criteria and transitions remain to be defined.
- Operational Knowledge Trust State must not be confused with the OKF lifecycle status of a documentation page.
- Draft Knowledge may be used immediately in an explicitly identified experimental workflow.
- Draft Knowledge must not directly contribute to official Coverage claims, project Pass/Fail decisions, official Test Packages, or approved Gate Baselines.
- Experimental execution and Feedback may provide Evidence for later promotion, while the official-use Trust threshold remains to be defined.
- The Knowledge producer and verifier must be independent; a generating Agent may not promote its own Knowledge to official Trust by itself.
- Automated evaluation may provide promotion Evidence, while initial final promotion authority belongs to the accountable expert for the target applicability scope.
- Broader automated promotion may be introduced later only after its evaluation and governance are proven.
- Knowledge with official use history must not be physically deleted or silently overwritten when found incorrect.
- The affected version is revoked from future use, a corrected version is created, and Traceability drives impact analysis and revalidation.
- Historical Gate Baselines, runs, Evidence, and verdicts remain reproducible while later invalidation, supersession, or correction relationships are appended.
